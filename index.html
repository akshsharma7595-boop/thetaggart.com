Diversified Portfolio!

Our trading and importation portfolio spans Steel, Ferrous & Non-Ferrous Metals, Technical Grade Urea, Sulphur, Gypsum, Fertilizers, Heavy Equipment, and Windmill Accessories.

Steel Products (Flat, Structural & Tubular) ⌄
Plates, Coils, Sheets, Pipes & Tubes (ERW, Seamless, Galvanized), Beams, Channels, Angles, Bars (Rounds, Squares, Flats, TMT Bars), and Wire Rods & Strips.
Ferrous, Non-Ferrous & Physical Precious Metals ⌄

Granular, Powdered, and Lump Sulphur, Natural Gypsum, Soda Ash, and specialized intermediate industrial chemicals sourced through our global partner network.
Fertilizers & Agricultural Inputs ⌄
Ammonium Sulphate, Ammonium Nitrate, Calcium Ammonium Nitrate (CAN), MOP, SOP, SSP, DAP, MAP, and custom NPK Compound Fertilizers.
